# AXT Inc (AXTI) Deep Dive Research Report

**Date:** 2026-03-26
**Ticker:** NASDAQ: AXTI
**Price:** ~$2.25 (as of late March 2026)
**Market Cap:** ~$97M

---

## Executive Summary

AXT Inc is a vertically integrated compound semiconductor substrate manufacturer, and the world's #2 InP (Indium Phosphide) substrate supplier behind Japan's Sumitomo Electric. InP is a critical bottleneck material for AI datacenter optical interconnects (800G/1.6T transceivers), making AXTI a potential beneficiary of the AI infrastructure buildout.

### Bull Case
- **Secular demand tailwind:** InP substrate demand growing 25-30% CAGR through 2030, driven by AI optical interconnect (800G->1.6T->3.2T transition)
- **Supply bottleneck:** Industry-wide 70% supply-demand gap; all major suppliers fully booked through 2026-2027
- **Vertical integration moat:** Only substrate maker that refines its own 7N (99.99999%) Indium from raw ore, giving 15-25% cost advantage
- **CPO catalyst:** Co-packaged optics (expected 2027-2028 volume) could 3-5x InP content per switch ASIC
- **Valuation:** Trading at 2.5x EV/Sales vs semiconductor peers at 5-8x; P/B of 0.58 (below book value)

### Bear Case
- **China export controls are existential:** Feb 2025 China export controls on InP require per-shipment permits (~60 business days). AXTI's manufacturing is 100% in China (Beijing Tongmei). Revenue already declining (Q4 2025 revenue $25.5M, down from $33M Q3).
- **Squeezed from both sides:**
  - Chinese customers shifting to domestic competitor **Yunnan Xinyao** (backed by Huawei's Hubble Investment, 23.91% stake)
  - International customers signing long-term deals with **Sumitomo** due to AXTI supply uncertainty (one customer signed 7-year deal)
- **Chinese competitor closing quality gap:** Yunnan Xinyao achieved EPD <300/cm^2 on 6-inch InP (approaching Sumitomo's benchmark), with 2026 production reportedly 100% pre-booked
- **Silicon photonics threat:** Intel/GlobalFoundries 300mm SiPh platforms could commoditize lower-end transceivers, limiting InP TAM growth
- **Cash burn:** Operating cash flow negative in recent quarters; $47.8M cash but declining

### Key Verdict
AXTI sits at the intersection of a massive secular trend (AI optical interconnect) and a severe geopolitical headwind (China export controls). The stock is cheap for a reason -- the China risk is real and worsening. The bull case requires either (a) export permit normalization, (b) successful capacity buildout outside China, or (c) a takeout at a premium to book value. The bear case sees continued revenue erosion as customers diversify away.
