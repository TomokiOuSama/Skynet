# 3. Chinese InP Competitors Analysis

## 3.1 Yunnan Xinyao (云南鑫耀半导体) -- THE Primary Threat

**Parent:** Yunnan Germanium (002428.SZ)
**This is AXT's most credible domestic Chinese competitor in InP substrates.**

### Current Status
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Current capacity | 150,000 wafers/year (2-4 inch) |
| 2026 target | 250,000-400,000 wafers/year (all sizes) |
| 6-inch EPD | <300/cm^2 (pilot line) |
| 6-inch yield | 72% (vs international best ~78-80%) |
| Total investment | RMB 323.67M ($46.25M) |
| Total raised | $59.2M |

### Key Investors
- **Hubble Investment (Huawei's investment arm): 23.91% stake** -- this is the most important fact
- Shenzhen Capital Group
- Shenzhen Capital Holdings

### Customer Commitments (Reported for 2026)
| Customer | Volume | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Huawei/HiSilicon | 120,000+ wafers | 40% prepayment, passed verification 2019 |
| Innolight (中际旭创) | 50,000 wafers | 6-inch focused, batch verified |
| Accelink (光迅科技) | 30,000 wafers | Mixed sizes |
| **Total** | **~200,000 wafers** | **2026 production reportedly 100% pre-booked** |

### 6-Inch Progress
- Completed "6-inch High-Quality InP Single Crystal Wafer Industrialization" project
- Pilot line: EPD ≤300/cm^2, yield 72%
- Small-batch trial production started Q4 2025
- Mass production transition expected Q1 2026 (reportedly 3 months ahead of schedule)

### Quality Assessment
- EPD <500/cm^2 in production (approaching Sumitomo's ~300 benchmark)
- Substrate uniformity deviation <3%
- Reportedly meets 1.6T optical module requirements
- **Gap:** 6-inch yield still 6-8 percentage points below international best-in-class
- **Caveat:** Yunnan Germanium itself cautioned "significant uncertainty from R&D to mass production"

### Competitive Advantage vs AXTI
- **Government backing** and policy tailwinds for domestic sourcing
- **Huawei captive demand** via Hubble investment relationship
- No export permit issues for Chinese customers
- 15-20% cheaper than imports

## 3.2 JFS Lab (九峰山实验室) -- R&D Platform, Not Direct Competitor

**Location:** Wuhan, Donghu High-tech Development Zone
**Role:** National-level R&D platform for compound semiconductors

**Important clarification:** JFS Lab is an epitaxial/device R&D platform, NOT a substrate manufacturer. It validates that domestic substrates (from Xinyao) can support advanced epitaxy.

**Key achievement (Aug 2025):**
- Successfully developed 6-inch InP-based PIN detector and FP laser epitaxial processes
- Used **domestic MOCVD equipment AND domestic InP substrates** (from Xinyao)
- First full domestic supply chain demonstration for large-size InP
- FP laser PL uniformity <1.5nm, PIN detector mobility >11,000 cm^2/Vs
- Plans to conquer 8-inch epitaxial technology by 2026

**Significance:** JFS Lab proves the domestic InP supply chain works end-to-end, reducing customer hesitation to switch from imported substrates.

## 3.3 Other Chinese InP Players

### Zhuhai Dingtai Xinyuan (珠海鼎泰芯源)
- Built China's first InP substrate production line with proprietary IP
- 30 patents (10 pending), crystal growth yield 40-50%
- Joint lab with Chinese Academy of Sciences since 2017
- Producing 2-inch to 6-inch wafers
- Smaller scale, not yet a major threat

### Huaxin Jingdian / Li'ang Jingdian (华芯晶电/立昂晶电)
- 4-inch InP substrate via VGF method, 70% yield at 50% import price
- Reportedly entered Apple's supply chain
- Optimizing large-size, low-dislocation substrates

### Sanan Optoelectronics (三安光电)
- Raised RMB 6.5B for expansion; Wuhan base: 10,000 6-inch wafers/month
- InP-based EML epitaxial wafer yield improved from 30% to 65%
- Products in Huawei's supply chain
- **Note:** Primarily epi/device player, not bare substrate competitor

### Vital Materials (先导稀材)
- Produces 2-4 inch InP substrates (Fe-doped, S-doped, Zn-doped)
- No evidence of 6-inch production or major expansion

### Shaanxi Yinjie (陕西铟杰半导体)
- Breakthrough in material preparation; stable mass production
- Government-supported domestic supply chain player

## 3.4 Quality Gap: Can Domestic InP Meet AI Datacenter Requirements?

**Short answer: Getting closer, but meaningful gap remains.**

| Parameter | International Best | Yunnan Xinyao | Other Domestic |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|----------------|
| EPD (6-inch) | ~300/cm^2 | <300/cm^2 (pilot) | >1,000/cm^2 |
| 6-inch yield | 78-80% | 72% | Unknown |
| Uniformity | <1.5% | <3% | Unknown |
| Wafer sizes | 2-6 inch | 2-6 inch | 2-4 inch mostly |

**Critical caveats:**
- Full-wafer EPD mapping needed for reliable qualification (not just sampling)
- As PIC complexity increases (1.6T generation), substrate quality becomes even more critical
- Semi-insulating InP for RF and highest-end laser applications not yet proven at scale domestically

## 3.5 Customer Switching Costs

**Typical qualification cycle:** 12-18 months (expanded from historical 6-9 months)

**Why switching is difficult:**
1. Epitaxial sensitivity -- new substrate means re-optimizing entire epi recipe
2. Crystal growth know-how -- each supplier's VGF process produces subtly different material
3. Limited alternatives -- only 3-4 suppliers at telecom grade
4. Certification stacking -- multi-stage qualification with major customers

**But switching IS happening in China because:**
1. Export control uncertainty makes AXT unreliable for Chinese customers
2. Government policy pressure for domestic sourcing
3. Huawei's strategic investment creates captive demand for Xinyao
4. Supply shortage -- customers will qualify new suppliers out of necessity
5. Cost advantage -- domestic substrates 15-20% cheaper

## 3.6 Key Takeaway

Yunnan Xinyao is a real and growing threat to AXTI's China business. With Huawei backing, approaching parity on quality metrics, and strong policy tailwinds, Xinyao is positioned to capture a significant share of China's InP substrate demand. The quality gap is narrowing but not yet closed for the most demanding applications.
