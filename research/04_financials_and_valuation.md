## 4.3 Valuation Analysis

### Current Multiples

| Metric | AXTI | Semi Peer Avg | Discount |
|--------|------|---------------|----------|
| EV/Sales | ~2.5x | 5-8x | 50-70% discount |
| P/B | 0.58x | 2-4x | 75-85% discount |
| P/E | N/A (negative) | 20-30x | N/A |
| EV/EBITDA | >50x | 15-25x | Premium (low earnings) |

### Why It's Cheap
1. **China export control overhang** -- market pricing in permanent impairment
2. **Negative/minimal earnings** -- no P/E valuation possible
3. **Revenue declining** -- Q4 2025 down 23% from Q3
4. **No clear path to resolution** -- management vague on alternatives

### Why It Might Be Too Cheap
1. **Below book value** ($2.25 vs $3.87 book) -- implies market values assets at 58 cents on the dollar
2. **InP demand is booming** -- structural shortage regardless of AXTI's issues
3. **Acquisition target** -- strategic value of InP technology, customer relationships, and VGF expertise
4. **Vertical integration** -- 7N Indium refining capability is rare and valuable

### Valuation Scenarios

| Scenario | Probability | Target Price | Return |
|----------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| **Bull:** Export controls ease, revenue recovers to $150M+ | 20% | $6-8 | +170-255% |
| **Base:** Muddling through, gradual revenue decline | 40% | $2-3 | -10% to +35% |
| **Bear:** Export controls tighten, revenue collapses | 25% | $1-1.50 | -33% to -55% |
| **Takeout:** Acquired at premium to book | 15% | $4-5 | +80-120% |

**Probability-weighted target:** ~$3.10 (38% upside from $2.25)

## 4.7 Key Takeaway

AXTI is cheap on paper (0.58x book, 2.5x EV/Sales) but cheap for valid reasons. The export control situation creates binary risk -- either it resolves and the stock re-rates significantly higher, or it worsens and the company faces structural decline. The acquisition scenario provides a potential floor near book value ($3.87). The stock is essentially a geopolitical option on US-China semiconductor relations.
