## 1.2 Supply-Demand Gap

The InP substrate market faces a **severe supply shortage**:
- Industry-wide **70% supply-demand gap** reported
- All major suppliers (Sumitomo, AXT/Tongmei, JX Metals) fully booked through 2026-2027
- Lead times extended from weeks to months
- DigiTimes (Dec 2025): "InP substrate shortages remain a bottleneck behind AI infrastructure"

## 1.3 AI Optical Interconnect Demand

### Current Generation (800G)
- Each 800G transceiver uses InP-based EML (electro-absorption modulated laser) or DFB laser chips
- Hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Meta, Amazon) deploying at massive scale
- Innolight, Coherent, Lumentum are key transceiver makers

### Next Generation (1.6T) -- 2025-2026 Ramp
- 1.6T transceivers require **more InP** per unit (4x 400G lanes vs 4x 200G)
- Higher-quality substrates needed (tighter EPD specs)
- Market consensus: 1.6T volume ramp H2 2025 through 2026

### Co-Packaged Optics (CPO) -- 2027-2028
- CPO integrates optical engines directly onto switch ASICs
- Could increase InP content **3-5x per switch** vs pluggable transceivers
- Broadcom, NVIDIA, and major switch ASIC vendors developing CPO
- Timeline: Engineering samples 2026, volume production 2027-2028
- **This is the biggest potential catalyst for InP demand**

### 3.2T and Beyond
- 3.2T expected 2028-2029 timeframe
- Will further increase InP substrate consumption
- May require 6-inch or larger wafers for cost efficiency

## 1.4 Silicon Photonics Threat

**The bear case technology risk:** Could SiPh replace InP?

**Current reality:**
- Intel and GlobalFoundries offer 300mm silicon photonics platforms
- SiPh advantages: leverages existing CMOS fabs, lower cost at scale, higher integration density
- SiPh limitations: **cannot generate light** -- still needs InP laser source (hybrid integration or external laser)

**Assessment:**
- For **short-reach** datacenter links (<500m): SiPh is competitive and gaining share
- For **long-haul** and coherent optics: InP remains essential, no viable replacement
- **Even SiPh platforms need InP lasers** -- this is a hybrid, not a replacement
- Net effect: SiPh may limit InP TAM growth in low-end segments but does NOT eliminate InP demand
- Industry consensus: InP and SiPh are **complementary**, not substitutional, for the foreseeable future

## 1.5 Key Takeaway

InP substrate demand is in a structural upcycle driven by AI infrastructure. The question for AXTI is not whether demand exists (it does, overwhelmingly) but whether AXTI can capture its share given geopolitical constraints.
